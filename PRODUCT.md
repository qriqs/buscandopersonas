# Product

BuscandoPersonas.org Sin Fronteras is a private investigation agency based in
Lima. Its main service helps people search for relatives and reconnect with
family. It also handles legal guidance and other private investigations.

The homepage helps someone with a sensitive or long-running search understand
the agency, consider what information they have, and choose a contact channel.
Visitors can use WhatsApp, the existing Jotform, telephone or email. The site
is in Peruvian Spanish.

The site uses the verified facts in
[the client brief](context/00-master-brief.md). The
[open questions](context/09-open-questions.md) identify operational claims,
permissions and policy details that still need confirmation from the agency.
