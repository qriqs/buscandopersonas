# BuscandoPersonas.org style lock

Follow [the design system](../DESIGN.md) for composition, typography, motion,
and source references. The homepage uses a warm editorial narrative with
archive photographs, a linked consultation, native disclosures, and agency
details.

## Color contract

The palette is defined in `src/styles/site.css`. Ink on paper measures
11.65:1, ink on sage measures 10.62:1, cream on forest measures 13.70:1,
and forest on lime measures 11.33:1. Hairlines on the light surfaces are
decorative. The text, plus icon and focus outline identify accordion controls
and their states.

## Asset roles

The archive photograph anchors the hero. The photograph of a hand holding a
print introduces the search steps. The original directors' portrait identifies
the business, and the original globe anchors the wordmark. The photographs in
the first two sections are illustrative. Use documented agency content for
any future case stories or testimonials.
