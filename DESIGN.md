# Design

The homepage follows a family search from a first clue to a private
conversation. The original gold globe sits alongside an editorial typography
pair, warm paper, forest green and photographs of family archives.

## Composition

The opening pairs a short two-line headline with one large photographic plate
and a clear WhatsApp action. The services section pairs a single family-search
feature with a compact, expandable list of additional services. The search
process combines a photograph with three numbered steps. The directors'
portrait provides a real point of identification for the agency.

On phones, prioritize headline, explanation and action, then show the
photograph. Keep the process, services, FAQ and contact options in a single
column at narrow widths. The compact menu and accordions use native disclosure
elements.

## Type and color

Instrument Serif is the display face. DM Sans is used for body copy,
navigation and controls. The typefaces are served locally.

The paper background is `#f6f3e9`, and the sage surface is `#e5ebdc`.
Forest `#182f26` and ink `#20362b` carry text and structure. Lime
`#dceda4` marks the primary action and headline emphasis on dark surfaces.
Use decorative hairlines for visual grouping and a high-contrast outline for
keyboard focus.

## Motion

Motion gently shifts the archive photo on scroll and draws the rule below the
search steps. Lenis smooths wheel scrolling on desktop. Touch, anchor links,
keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion preferences retain native scrolling.

## References

Oil UI's Sora demo informed the use of a single visual world from the cover
through the lower sections. Build with Taste's TP-7 Perceptions cover informed
the use of a dominant object, quiet type hierarchy and one accent color.
