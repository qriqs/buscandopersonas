# 04 — Brand, voice and visual identity

**Status:** the brand exists in the founder's material but not as a coherent system. The redesign has to consolidate it, not invent a new one from zero.

---

## 1. The name problem

The business uses at least eight names interchangeably:

| Variant | Where it appears | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| **BuscandoPersonas.Org** | domain, logo, copyright | The canonical written form, but capitalisation varies |
| **Buscandopersonas.org SIN FRONTERAS** | Facebook page name | The main public-facing name |
| **BUSCAPERSONAS SIN FRONTERAS** | Facebook description | A misspelling of "Buscando" |
| **BuscandoPersonas.ORG Buscandote** | second Facebook page | Legacy page; "Buscandote" is also a brand |
| **Buscándote** | hero heading and old site | Used as a product/agency name |
| **Busca Personas Sin Fronteras** | TikTok display name | Simplified for social |
| **Buscandote Sin Fronteras** | co-director's profile | Personal brand |
| **Agencia de Investigaciones Buscándote** | old site | Legal/trading name candidate |
| **Grupo de Investigaciones Especiales BUSCANDOTE (GIEB)** | 2012–2013 site | Internal unit name |
| **Unidad de Investigación Especial Buscandot (UIEB)** | 2013 site | Internal unit name |

**Implication:** a visitor may see four different names across Google, Facebook, TikTok and the site and wonder whether they are the same organisation. A redesign must pick **one primary name**, one short form, and explain the legacy names once (e.g. "antes Buscándote").

**Recommendation (for the owner to decide):**
- Primary: **BuscandoPersonas.org**
- Descriptor: **Sin Fronteras**
- Short: **BuscandoPersonas**
- Retire: "BUSCAPERSONAS", "Buscandote" as primary names, except in a historical note.

## 3. Voice

### Attributes

| Attribute | What it sounds like | What it must avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** | "Sabemos que esta decisión no es fácil." | Cold, clinical, bureaucratic |
| **Respectful** | "Su historia será tratada con reserva." | Judgmental, moralising |
| **Hopeful, not magical** | "Vamos a evaluar su caso con honestidad." | "Garantizado", "100%", "siempre" |
| **Human** | "Cuéntenos quién es esa persona para usted." | Corporate jargon, "soluciones integrales" |
| **Professional** | "Investigadores, abogados y trabajadores sociales." | Detective clichés, macho bravado |
| **Faith-friendly** | "Que Dios los bendiga" is authentic in testimonials | Preaching, excluding non-religious clients |
| **Clear** | Short sentences, concrete next steps | Long legalistic paragraphs |

### Tone by context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Emotional, direct, hopeful | "Hay personas que nunca dejaron de estar en tu corazón. Vamos a buscarlas." |
| Family search page | Empathetic, patient, honest | "No importa cuánto tiempo haya pasado. Empezamos por escuchar tu historia." |
| Corporate page | Sober, precise, discreet | "Investigaciones internas con evidencia admisible y absoluta reserva." |
| Infidelity page | Calm, non-judgmental, evidence-focused | "Si necesitas saber la verdad, podemos obtener pruebas legales y discretas." |
| Legal page | Clear, reassuring | "Te orientamos sobre tus derechos y los pasos que puedes dar." |
| Case stories | Narrative, respectful, consent-aware | "Después de 35 años, Juan abrazó a su padre." |
| Error/empty/grief states | Gentle, human | "No pudimos encontrar a esta persona todavía. Hablemos de lo que sí sabemos." |

### Do

- Address the visitor as **tú** or **usted** consistently. The current site mixes both ("Registre su caso" + "Cuéntanos tu caso"). For a Peruvian audience and a sensitive topic, **usted** is safer on family-search pages; **tú** can work on social/TikTok. Pick one per page.
- Use **"persona"** and **"ser querido"**, not "objetivo" or "blanco".
- Say **"búsqueda"**, **"ubicación"**, **"reencuentro"**, **"reunión"**, **"historia"**.
- Use **"confidencialidad"** and explain it concretely.
- Use **"investigadores"**, **"equipo"**, **"abogados"**, **"trabajadores sociales"**.
- Use real numbers only when sourced.

### Don't

- "100% efectividad", "garantizado", "siempre encontramos".
- "El irresponsable" / "la persona que te engaña" (judgmental).
- "Detective privado" as the main identity (it narrows and cheapens the emotional product).
- "Spy", "rastrear", "hackear", "sin dejar rastro" (illegal connotation).
- "Barato", "precio increíble", "promoción" (devalues a sensitive service).
- ALL CAPS for whole paragraphs. The current site overuses it.
- Religious exclusivity ("Dios" in every headline). Keep it in testimonials and human moments.

### Reading level

The audience includes elderly people, people with limited formal education, and non-native Spanish speakers. Aim for:
- Sentences under 20 words.
- Common words; explain "viabilidad", "jurisdicción", "cadena de custodia".
- One idea per paragraph.
- Subheads that answer a question.

## 5. Visual identity

### The mark

- A **gold globe/planet** made of a wireframe sphere with small figures or a tree inside. It appears in:
  - the hero graphic (top right),
  - the team collage (centre),
  - the old site header.
- It reads as **world / union / humanity / gold / precious**.
- It is the most distinctive brand asset. **The current site does not use it at all** (the logo file is missing; the nav logo is commented out).

### Palette

**From the brand's own imagery:**
- **Gold** — the globe, warmth, value, reunion.
- **Navy / royal blue** — trust, seriousness, the "Lo Ubicamos Por ti" lettering.
- **White** — clarity, breathing room.
- **Warm red / coral** — family clothing in the photos; use sparingly as an accent.
- **DNA cyan** — the helix; a scientific accent, not a primary colour.

**From the current CSS (generic template):**
- Primary: `#16df7e` (bright green)
- Hover: `#206bfb` (bright blue)
- Text: `#444444`
- Muted: `#5f687b`
- Dark mode: `#18191a`, `#242526`, `#3a3b3c`
- Light gray: `#f9f9fa`

**Assessment:** the green is unrelated to the gold globe and the navy/blue photography. It makes the site look like a generic 2020 Bootstrap template. The redesign should derive a palette from the globe: **navy, royal blue, gold, warm white**, with a restrained accent.

### Typography (current)

- **Open Sans** — body.
- **Raleway** — headings.
- **Poppins** — some headings.

All three are Google Fonts and generic. For a trust-and-warmth brand, consider a humanist sans for body and a slightly editorial serif or a strong grotesk for headlines. (Design decision for the redesign team; not prescribed here.)

### Photography and art direction

**What exists:**
- `assets/img/hero.webp` — a composite graphic: "Lo Ubicamos Por ti", a family embrace, a group of children, two elderly women embracing, a DNA helix, a gold globe, and the list "Padres biológicos / Hijos-Familiares / Antepasados / Amigos-Conocidos".
- `assets/img/about.png` — a collage of nine team portraits around the gold globe on white, with drop shadows. Amateur but authentic.
- `assets/img/team/ceos.jpg` — two men in suits shaking hands outdoors. Low resolution, dated, but real.
- Testimonial images — stock portraits (see `06`), not real clients.
- No case photography, no office photography, no documents, no behind-the-scenes.

**What the brand needs:**
- **Real people, real reunions** (with consent) — the single most powerful asset.
- **The team at work** — desks, maps, documents, field visits (careful with client confidentiality).
- **The directors** — a proper portrait, names, roles, bios.
- **The office / address** — proof of physical presence.
- **Documents and maps** — texture of real investigation, without exposing client data.
- **The gold globe** — as a quiet brand signature.

**What to avoid:**
- Generic stock "detective with binoculars" imagery.
- Hooded hackers, magnifying glasses, trench coats.
- AI-generated families (the audience is alert to fakeness).
- Overly polished corporate imagery that contradicts the human story.
- Images of children without consent.

## 6. Brand personality

If the brand were a person:

> A seasoned former police investigator who has seen a lot, speaks quietly, dresses simply, keeps his word, and cries a little when a family reunites. He is not a showman. He answers the phone at 11 pm. He says "Dios te bendiga" and means it. He will tell you the truth even when it hurts.

**Archetype:** the **Guardian** (protection, discretion, duty) with a strong **Everyman** warmth. Not the Hero, not the Magician, not the Ruler.

**Values:**
- Family.
- Discretion.
- Persistence.
- Justice.
- Faith / hope.
- Professionalism.
- Humanity.

**Avoid:**
- "Busca personas" as a noun (awkward).
- "Cazadores de personas" / "rastreadores".
- "Detectives" as the hero label.
- "Terapias" unless clinical therapy is actually offered. The correct frame is **acompañamiento** (accompaniment), **orientación** (guidance), **contención emocional** (emotional support).

## 9. What the brand is *not*

- Not a police force or government agency.
- Not a charity, even though it does humanitarian work.
- Not a "paranormal" or "spy" brand.
- Not a luxury boutique detective agency.
- Not a generic digital people-search tool.
- Not a law firm.

The brand sits in a rare position: **professional investigation with a human heart**. That tension is the identity. The design should hold both — the seriousness of evidence and the warmth of a family reunion.
