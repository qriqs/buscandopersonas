# 02 — Services

The current website lists six services, all with equal visual weight. The real business has **one flagship service** (family search and reunion) and a set of **commercial investigation lines**. The new site should reflect that hierarchy rather than treating "Infidelidades" and "Buscar a mi padre" as equivalent.

Below, each service is documented with: who it is for, the trigger, what the business promises, what the client receives, the exact published copy, and what is still unknown.

# 1. Búsqueda y ubicación de personas
### Family search, missing persons & reunion

**One-line promise:** We find the person who is missing from your life and help you reunite — no matter how far away they are or how many years have passed.

**Who it is for:**
- Adults who grew up without a biological parent and want to find them.
- Parents looking for a child they lost contact with, gave up, or who was taken.
- Siblings separated by migration, family conflict, divorce, abandonment or war.
- Grandchildren and relatives looking for an ancestor or a family branch.
- People looking for an ex-partner, childhood friend, schoolmate or acquaintance.
- Families of a missing person — including cases of forced disappearance, conflict, or a relative who left home and never returned.
- Members of the Peruvian diaspora, and foreigners with family ties to Peru.

**The trigger:** a birthday, a death in the family, a DNA test result, a letter found in a drawer, a childhood memory, a health issue, a inheritance matter, or simply "I need to know before it's too late."

**What they promise:**
> "Ubicamos padres biológicos, familiares, hermanos, hijos, amigos, conocidos, antepasados… aunque haya transcurrido el tiempo y lugar de los hechos."

> "BUSCAS A TUS PADRES BIOLÓGICOS, familiares, hermanos, hijos, antepasados, exnovios, conocidos etc. sin importar el LUGAR ni el TIEMPO que haya transcurrido. EN ESPAÑOL, INGLÉS e ITALIANO."

> "Nuestros expertos INVESTIGADORES resuelven casos que otros no pueden."

**How they do it (as described across sources):**
- A detailed interview and case history.
- Identity and data work: names, documents, dates, places, last known addresses; old material explicitly names **RENIEC** (the Peruvian civil registry).
- Field investigation in urban, marginal and rural areas; staff who speak indigenous languages where needed (2013 claim).
- A network of collaborators, government institutions and private organisations in Peru and abroad.
- Forensic identification where identity is uncertain: papiloscopy, fingerprint teratology, quiroscopy, pelmatoscopy, odontology, facial identification.
- DNA testing as a confirmation tool (mentioned in the hero graphic and old copy).
- Legal channels and, where relevant, lawyers.
- Mediation of the first contact and support around the reunion.

**What the client receives (inferred from case stories, not a published package):**
- Confirmation of the person's identity and whereabouts.
- A report or explanation of the findings.
- Help making contact — phone call, message, or an in-person reunion.
- Emotional preparation and, where needed, legal orientation.
- In some cases, the family is put in touch with the person's current family.

**Published case outcomes (examples):**
- "Después de 35 años conoce a su padre."
- "Después de 17 años encuentra a su hermano."
- "Después de 28 años encuentra a su madre."
- "Después de 25 años regresa a casa."
- "Se reunió con su papá 20 años después."
- "Mi medio hermano se reunió con su papá 20 años después."
- "Encontró a su hermano y pudo estar toda la familia completa."
- "Lo buscaban hace 22 años" — a man living on the streets of Lima, reunited with his family in Cajamarca.
- A father in Chile who had never met his father; a Colombian man wandering in Arequipa; a family separated by Sendero Luminoso in 1993.

**Emotional territory:** hope, guilt, fear of rejection, fear of what will be found, loyalty to adoptive or current family, grief when the person has died, relief, closure. The copy must hold all of these without promising a happy ending.

**What is unknown:**
- Success rate, average time, price, geographic limits.
- Whether they charge if the person is not found.
- Whether they contact the person first or the client first.
- How they handle a refusal by the person sought.
- Whether they work with minors and under what safeguards.
- Whether they can access official records directly or only through lawyers/contacts.

**Website role:** the hero, the primary navigation item, the main case-story section, and the main conversion path. Everything else supports it.

# 2. Asesoría jurídica
### Legal advice around the search

**One-line promise:** Legal orientation so the reunion — and any rights that depend on it — can happen safely.

**Who it is for:** families facing child support, inheritance, recognition of paternity, adoption, guardianship, or a legal obstacle to contact.

**What the old site says:**
> "El personal de abogados que pertenece a nuestra comunidad… se encuentra presto a cualquier solicitud; si el caso lo amerita, con respecto a un caso especial, la asesoría legal estará gustoso de informarle y darle viabilidad a sus solicitudes con la finalidad de prevalecer sus derechos para con sus seres queridos."

A Facebook post from the co-director's profile promises:
> "ASESORÍA GRATUITA PARA INICIAR DEMANDA POR ALIMENTOS."

**What is known:** lawyers are part of the team; legal advice is offered; the old site had an "ASESORIA/JURIDICA" page (2015) but it contained no body copy.

**What is unknown:** whether legal advice is included, billable, or referred; which firm; which jurisdictions.

**Website role:** a short section under family search ("we can also help with the legal side") and a separate page if it is a real, staffed service. Do not overbuild it without confirmation.

# 3. Infidelidades
### Infidelity investigations

**One-line promise:** Solid, discreet evidence — photos, videos, documents — if you need the truth for a decision or a court case.

**Who it is for:** spouses or partners who suspect infidelity and need certainty or evidence.

**Current website copy:**
> "Evidencia sólida (fotos/videos/documentos) para juicio. Confidencialidad garantizada."

**Older copy (2015):**
> "¿Sospechas de una infidelidad? Obtén la verdad que necesitas. Nuestras investigaciones discretas te brindarán las respuestas que buscas."

> "¿Desea conocer con quién la engaña? Plenamente lo identificamos."

**What they likely deliver:** surveillance, photographic/video evidence, identification of the third party, a report usable in legal proceedings, confidentiality.

**Sensitivities:** privacy law, entrapment, illegal recording, family violence. The copy should promise **lawful** evidence and discretion, not voyeurism. Avoid moralising or sensationalising.

**What is unknown:** price, timeframe, whether they testify in court, whether they operate outside Peru.

**Website role:** a discreet, secondary service page. Tone: calm, adult, non-judgmental, evidence-focused.

# 4. Deudores por alimentos
### Child-support debtor location

**One-line promise:** We find the parent who is not paying and give you the information you need to enforce your child's rights.

**Who it is for:** mothers, fathers or guardians with a child-support order or a claim in progress; lawyers who need the debtor's address or workplace.

**Current website copy:**
> "Localización de personas para manutención. Información para demanda por alimentos. Consulta confidencial."

**Older copy:**
> "Asesoría jurídica y procesal, ubicamos al irresponsable, su centro laboral y domicilio actual."

**What they deliver:** current address, workplace, and possibly other information needed to file or enforce a child-support claim; legal orientation.

**Sensitivities:** the language "el irresponsable" is judgmental; a modern site can be firm without insulting. The service is emotionally charged and often involves children — privacy and dignity matter.

**What is unknown:** whether they file the claim, work with a lawyer, or only locate; price; coverage.

**Website role:** a dedicated page with a clear "what you receive" list and a hand-off to legal advice.

**Who it is for:** vehicle owners, insurers, companies with fleets, lawyers handling a judicial recovery.

**What is unknown:** the current 24-hour numbers, legal scope, insurance partnerships, pricing.

# 6. Estafadores
### Fraudster location

**One-line promise:** We identify and locate the person who defrauded you, through legal and effective methods, so you can recover what you lost.

**Who it is for:** individuals and small businesses who have been defrauded and need the person's identity or whereabouts.

**Current website copy:**
> "Localización de personas a nivel nacional. Métodos legales y efectivos. Confidencialidad."

**Older copy:**
> "No te dejes engañar. Identificamos y localizamos a estafadores para que puedas recuperar lo perdido."

The old site also published an educational article on how scammers operate, which suggests content marketing potential.

**What they deliver:** identification, location, evidence, a report for a lawyer or the police.

**Sensitivities:** presumption of innocence; the service should not promise recovery of money; coordinate with legal authorities.

**Website role:** a service page paired with practical fraud-prevention content.

**What is unknown:** whether they have a dedicated cyber unit, tools, or certified forensic examiners.

**Website role:** a service page; could be grouped with "Estafadores" under a "Fraud & digital" pillar.

# 8. Robo sistemático en empresas
### Corporate internal theft investigations

**One-line promise:** We investigate internal theft and identify those responsible with evidence that stands up in court.

**Who it is for:** SMEs, retailers, warehouses, family businesses, and legal/compliance teams.

**Current website copy:**
> "Investigaciones internas (actos ilícitos en su empresa). Identificación de responsables. Protección de intereses y cumplimiento normativo."

**Older copy:**
> "Las empresas tienen en sus almacenes los productos que de una u otra forma sus empleados con mucha sutileza roban sistemáticamente en contubernio. Asimismo, los ejecutivos de la empresa malversan los presupuestos para sus intereses y de terceros. La capacidad de respuesta ante estos hechos y la investigación prolija que realiza nuestro plantel de investigadores determinarán al autor intelectual y material con pruebas irrefutables para los órganos jurisdiccionales."

> "La informática forense… puede ayudar a resolver grandes crímenes apoyándose en el método científico, aplicado a la recolección, análisis y validación de todo tipo de pruebas digitales."

**Sensitivities:** labour law, employee privacy, defamation, chain of custody. The site should signal legality and proportionality.

**Website role:** B2B page with a "request a confidential consultation" CTA.

# 9. Consulta de datos / people lookup
### People-data lookup (partner / B2B)

**Evidence:** the brand appears in posts on Facebook pages such as **"Ecuadorlegal"** with copy like:
> "¿Buscas alguna persona? Consulta por nombres o número."
> "Descubre los datos de una persona de forma rápida y sencilla."
> "Con total discreción y confidencialidad. Servicio nacional e internacional."
> "Escríbenos a: contacto@buscandopersonas.org — Contáctanos: +51 01 7548441."

**Interpretation [LIKELY]:** a lower-ticket lead-generation offer for people-search/data lookup, distinct from a full investigation. It may be sold through partner legal pages in Ecuador and Peru.

**Risks:** data-protection law, accuracy, consent, and reputational overlap with "background check" services. If this is a real service, it needs its own transparent terms and lawful basis. If it is only lead-gen, do not build it into the main site.
