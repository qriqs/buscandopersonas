# Context pack — BuscandoPersonas.org

**Prepared:** 27 November 2026
**Purpose:** Give a redesign team (human or AI) everything it needs to understand the business before writing a single line of website code. This pack is **context only** — no design decisions, no new copy for the site, no code.

**Sources scraped:** the live website `buscandopersonas.org`, the Facebook page `BuscandoPersonasSinFronteras`, the TikTok account, the older WordPress site via the Wayback Machine, public directory listings, and web search results. Every claim is tied to a source in `10-sources.md`; raw captures are in `raw/`.

## Confidence labels used throughout

- **[FACT]** — stated by the business itself or directly observable (e.g. a phone number on the live site).
- **[LIKELY]** — supported by more than one public source, but not confirmed by the owner.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** — appears in old or inconsistent material; do not publish without confirmation.
- **[GAP]** — unknown/publicly undisclosed. Listed in `09-open-questions.md`.
- **[RISK]** — a claim, placeholder, or practice that could damage trust or create legal exposure if repeated.

---

## Three rules for the redesign team

1. **Do not repeat unverified numbers or placeholders.** The current site claims several different case counts, uses template testimonial names ("Saul Goodman", "John Larson", "Jena Karlis") and a fake portfolio ("Caso 1", "Web 3", "App 2"). All of that must be replaced with verified material or removed.
2. **Treat this as a sensitive, trust-first category.** Visitors are often grieving, ashamed, scared of being scammed, or worried about the privacy of the person they are looking for. The site must reassure before it sells.
3. **The core business is family search and reunion, not "private detective" clichés.** Infidelity, fraud and corporate work are real revenue lines, but the emotional centre — and the public identity — is "unimos familias donde la adversidad impuso distancia."

## What this pack deliberately does not contain

- Prices, packages, refund policy, contract terms — never published.
- Legal entity type, RUC, registration — not confirmed.
- Real staff bios beyond the two directors' names and Martín Arias García's public background.
- Consent status for using the published case stories or client photos.
- Guarantees about timelines or success rates that the business has not authorised.

Those are tracked in `09-open-questions.md`.
