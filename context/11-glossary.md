# Glossary — Spanish terms used by the business

For the redesign team. These are the words the audience and the organisation actually use. Keep them consistent across the site.

---

## Organisations and roles

| Term | Meaning | Notes for copy |
|---|---|---|
| **PIP** | Policía de Investigaciones del Perú | Former national investigative police, merged into the PNP in 2001. "Ex PIP" signals investigative experience; it does not mean current police authority. |
| **PNP** | Policía Nacional del Perú | The current national police. Used in vehicle-recovery copy ("Dirección de Robos Vehicular de la PNP"). |
| **RENIEC** | Registro Nacional de Identificación y Estado Civil | Peru's civil registry. Old copy says they use it. Never imply unauthorised access. |
| **Fiscalía** | Public Prosecutor's Office | Where evidence/reports may be sent. |
| **Serenazgo** | Municipal security service | Used in missing-person bulletins ("llame a la Policía o al Serenazgo"). |
| **Poder Judicial** | Judiciary | Courts. |
| **GIEB** | Grupo de Investigaciones Especiales Buscándote | Legacy internal unit name (2012–2013). |
| **UIEB** | Unidad de Investigación Especial Buscandot | Legacy internal unit name (2013). |

## Legal and family terms

| Term | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Deudor por alimentos** | A parent who owes child support | The current site says "el irresponsable" — judgmental. Prefer "la persona obligada" or "el progenitor". |
| **Demanda por alimentos** | Child-support lawsuit | A legal process; the agency may locate the person and coordinate with a lawyer. |
| **Pensión de alimentos** | Child-support payment | Common search term. |
| **Tenencia** | Custody | Adjacent legal matter. |
| **Patria potestad** | Parental authority | Adjacent. |
| **Reconocimiento de paternidad** | Paternity recognition | Often follows a search. |
| **Herencia** | Inheritance | A reason to look for a missing relative. |
| **Notificación** | Legal notification | Lawyers need an address to notify. |
| **Mandato judicial** | Court order | Vehicle recovery requires judicial authorisation. |
| **Suplantación de identidad** | Identity impersonation | Current site's cyber service. |
| **Estafa** | Fraud/scam | "Estafadores" = fraudsters. |
| **Delito informático** | Cybercrime | Current site uses "Delitos Infortmáticos" (typo). |
| **Robo sistemático** | Systematic theft | Internal, repeated theft in a company. |
| **Caso resuelto** | Resolved case | Define what "resolved" means before using the number. |

## Tone and register

| Term | Use |
|---|---|
| **Usted** | Safer for the sensitive family-search pages; formal, respectful. |
| **Tú** | Can work on social/TikTok and younger audiences; pick one per page. |
| **"Ser querido"** | Warm, inclusive of non-family relationships. |
| **"Persona buscada"** | Neutral, respectful. Avoid "objetivo". |
| **"Familiar ausente"** | Used in their own copy; gentle. |
| **"La pieza ausente"** | Their metaphor for the missing person; powerful but use sparingly. |
| **"Volver a casa"** | Recurring emotional frame. |
| **"Dios los bendiga"** | Authentic in testimonials; do not force it into every headline. |

## Historical/technical terms you may see in old copy

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **RPC** | "Red Privada de Comunicaciones" — old Peruvian mobile radio/phone service. |
| **Nextel** | Old push-to-talk mobile network; numbers like `41*159*1873`. |
| **Jirón** | A street/block in Lima's historic centre (e.g. Jirón Lampa). |
| **Cercado de Lima** | Lima's central district. |
| **Cono Norte** | Northern cone of Lima (districts such as Comas, Independencia). |
| **Asentamiento humano** | Informal settlement / shantytown. |
| **Caserío** | Hamlet/rural settlement. |
| **Provincia** | Province; in Peru also used for "the interior, outside Lima". |
| **Sendero Luminoso** | Shining Path, the Maoist insurgency; appears in a 1993 case. Handle with historical care. |

## Words to avoid

| Avoid | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| **"El irresponsable"** | Judgmental, hostile | "El progenitor obligado" / "la persona obligada" |
| **"Cazar" / "rastrear"** | Predatory, illegal connotation | "Ubicar" / "buscar" |
| **"Espía" / "espionaje"** | Illegal/cheap connotation | "Investigación" / "seguimiento" |
| **"Hackear"** | Illegal | "Análisis forense digital" |
| **"100% efectividad"** | Unsubstantiated | "Evaluación honesta" / "compromiso" |
| **"Garantizado"** | Legally risky | "Trabajamos para…" |
| **"Terapias"** | Implies clinical therapy | "Acompañamiento" / "orientación" / "contención emocional" |
| **"Barato"** | Devalues a sensitive service | "Honorarios accesibles" / "consulta sin costo" (if true) |
