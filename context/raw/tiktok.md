## Assessment

**Strengths**
- The 35-year reunion story is a strong short-form hook.
- The account uses real footage and family voices, not stock.
- The "PARTE FINAL" format creates serialized tension.

**Weaknesses**
- 163 followers is small; the account is not yet a discovery engine.
- Captions mix Spanish and English metadata; inconsistent.
- No visible CTA to the website or WhatsApp in the captured text.
- Some content (cremation question) is tangential to the brand.
- No consistent series naming or thumbnail system.

**Opportunity**
- Turn each resolved case into a 3-part series: the separation, the search, the reunion.
- Add a consistent end card: "¿Buscas a alguien? Escríbenos por WhatsApp."
- Cross-post the best TikToks as Facebook reels (already partially done).
- Link to a dedicated `/historias/` page, not the home page.
