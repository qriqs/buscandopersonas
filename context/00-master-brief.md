# BuscandoPersonas.org — Master context brief

**Snapshot:** 27 Nov 2026 · **Language of the business:** Spanish (Peru) · **Also claims:** English, Italian

---

## 1. What this business is, in one paragraph

BuscandoPersonas.org — publicly branded **"Buscandopersonas.org SIN FRONTERAS"** — is a Lima-based private investigation agency with a humanitarian specialisation in **finding people and reuniting families**. It is run by two directors, **Adolfo (Ramírez Funes)** and **Martín (Arias García)**, and its public story is built on a team of former officers of the **Policía de Investigaciones del Perú (PIP)**, lawyers, teachers, social workers and university graduates. Its core promise is to locate biological parents, children, siblings, ancestors, ex-partners, friends and missing persons **"sin importar el lugar ni el tiempo transcurrido"** — regardless of distance or how many years have passed — in Spanish, English and Italian. Around that emotional core it sells conventional private-investigation services: infidelity evidence, location of child-support debtors, vehicle recovery, fraudster location, cyber-impersonation cases, and internal theft investigations for companies.

## 2. The offer

### Core / flagship
1. **Búsqueda y ubicación de personas** — family search and reunion (biological parents, children, siblings, ancestors, ex-partners, friends, missing persons).
2. **Asesoría jurídica** — legal guidance connected to the search (child support, inheritance, family matters).

### Commercial investigation lines
3. **Infidelidades** — evidence for court, absolute confidentiality.
4. **Deudores por alimentos** — locate the debtor, their workplace and current address; support the legal claim.
5. **Ubicación y recuperación de vehículos** — nationwide, with judicial authorisation; "al 100%, vale decir al éxito."
6. **Estafadores** — locate fraudsters nationally through legal methods.
7. **Delitos informáticos** — identity impersonation on social media, reputation protection, for individuals and companies.
8. **Robo sistemático en empresas** — internal investigations, identify those responsible, evidence for prosecutors, compliance.

### Adjacent / legacy capabilities
9. **People-data lookup** — "consulta por nombres o número" promoted through partner legal pages; possibly B2B.
10. **Forensic identification** — papiloscopy, fingerprint/teratology, quiroscopy, pelmatoscopy, odontology, facial identification, DNA testing. Presented on the old site as scientific credibility; barely visible on the current one.

## 3. Who it serves

- **Adult children looking for a biological parent** — the single most common story in their public case archive.
- **Parents looking for a child** who left, was taken, was given up, or disappeared.
- **Siblings and extended family** separated by migration, conflict, abandonment or decades of silence.
- **Families of missing persons** — including cases linked to Peru's internal conflict and to migration.
- **Estranged partners, friends and acquaintances** trying to reconnect.
- **The Peruvian diaspora and foreigners with Peruvian ties** — Chile, Argentina, Ecuador, Mexico, the United States, Canada, Italy.
- **Family lawyers and notaries** who need an address or whereabouts for child support, inheritance or notification.
- **Spouses or partners** suspecting infidelity.
- **Fraud victims** and **SMEs** facing internal theft or impersonation.

The emotional job is not "hire a detective". It is: *"Help me close a wound I have carried for years, without exposing me or the person I am looking for."*

## 4. How a case works today

1. **First contact** — WhatsApp (`+51 999 934 443`), the JotForm on the website, Facebook Messenger, or a phone call (`+51 1 754 8441`).
2. **Intake** — the form asks for: full name, nationality, phone, email, **the name of the person sought**, the story of the case, and optional photos/documents.
3. **Feasibility & quote** — private, not published. Presumably an assessment of available data and a case fee.
4. **Investigation** — database checks (RENIEC is named in old material), field work, the collaborator network, legal channels, and — where needed — forensic identification or DNA.
5. **Location & approach** — confirming identity, then making contact with the person sought, respecting their willingness.
6. **Reunion** — phone or in-person, with emotional and legal support around the reunion.
7. **Follow-up** — testimonial or public case story, usually with the family's participation.

**Publicly unknown:** pricing, payment structure, timelines, success guarantees, contract terms, refund policy, data-retention policy, what happens if the person does not want to be found.

## 9. Redesign implications (context, not prescription)

1. **Lead with the reunion, not the detective.** The strongest asset is the emotional transformation: "volver a casa", "conocer a mi padre después de 35 años".
2. **Separate the two businesses clearly.** Family search needs warmth, privacy and hope. Corporate/fraud/infidelity needs discretion, evidence and speed. One brand can hold both, but the journeys should not be mixed.
3. **Prove trust in the first screen.** Real names, real roles, the 2014 page history, the ex-PIP background, the address, the languages, a clear process, and a privacy promise. Remove every placeholder.
4. **Make the intake feel safe.** Explain what happens after sending the form, who sees it, how confidentiality works, and what it costs to ask.
5. **Respect the person being sought.** Publish an ethical stance: no harassment, consent before contact, no publication of a located person's data, sensitivity to minors and to families where contact may be unwanted.
6. **Fix the facts.** One canonical set of numbers; no "100%" unless substantiated; a single phone number strategy; consistent NAP (name, address, phone).
7. **Localise properly.** If English and Italian are real service languages, build those pages; if not, stop claiming them.
8. **Design for mobile and low bandwidth.** The audience is largely in Peru and the diaspora, often on WhatsApp-first phones.
9. **Add the missing trust pages:** About/team, How it works, Confidentiality & privacy, FAQ, Pricing or "request a quote", Case stories (consented), Reviews, Legal notice, Complaints/ethics.
10. **Keep the gold globe and navy/blue/gold palette.** The current green template is generic and unrelated to the brand's own imagery.
