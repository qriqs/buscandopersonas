# 09 — Open questions for the owner

**Do not invent answers to these.** They are the facts the redesign cannot safely assume. Group them by who can answer and use them as an interview guide.

---

## A. Identity and legal structure (ask first)

1. **What is the legal name** of the organisation, and what is the RUC (if in Peru)?
2. Is it a **company, an NGO, an association, or a sole proprietorship**? The directory says NGO; Facebook says "Entrepreneur".
3. **When was it founded?** The site says 2002, but the earliest public evidence is 2012. Which is correct?
4. Who are the **current directors/owners**, and what are their full names and roles? ("Adolfo y Martín" — full surnames?)
5. Is **"Buscándote"** a separate legal entity, a former name, or an internal unit?
6. Which **trademarks** are registered, if any?
7. What is the **relationship between the main Facebook page, the legacy page, and Martín Arias García's personal profile**? Which is canonical?
8. Are there **other offices** beyond the Lima address? Which countries can you legally operate in?
9. Do you have **professional liability insurance**?
10. Are you registered with any **association of private investigators** or similar body?

---

## B. Services and scope

11. Which services are **currently active and sold**? Which are legacy?
12. Is **family search** the main revenue line, or is it a humanitarian arm subsidised by the investigation work?
13. Do you still offer **forensic identification** (papiloscopy, fingerprints, odontology, facial identification)? In-house or partners?
14. Do you offer **DNA testing**? Which lab? Is it informative or legal/admissible?
15. Do you handle **adoption searches** and **origin searches**? Under what legal framework?
16. Do you take **missing-person cases** that are also police cases? How do you coordinate with the PNP/Fiscalía?
17. Do you work on **forced disappearance / internal conflict** cases? Any restrictions?
18. Do you handle **minors**? What safeguards and parental consents apply?
19. Do you handle **cross-border cases**? Which countries, and through what partners?
20. Do you offer **legal representation**, or only orientation? Which lawyer/firm?
21. Is **"consulta por nombres o número"** a real service, a lead-gen offer, or a partner promotion?
22. Do you offer **corporate/background checks**? Are they lawful under Peruvian data protection law?
23. What is the **smallest / largest case** you accept?
24. What cases do you **decline**?

---

## C. Process and operations

25. What happens **after someone sends the form or WhatsApp message**? Who replies, how fast, in what language?
26. What is the **average response time**? Is there 24/7 coverage?
27. What is the **intake process**? Is there a call, a questionnaire, a document checklist?
28. How do you assess **feasibility**? What databases/tools do you use lawfully?
29. What is the **typical timeline** for a family search? A corporate case? A vehicle recovery?
30. How often do you **update the client**? Who is the point of contact?
31. What do you deliver at the end — a **written report, a call, a reunion, a certificate**?
32. What happens if the person is **not found**? Do you refund, continue, or close?
33. What happens if the person is **found but refuses contact**?
34. What happens if the person is **deceased**? How do you confirm and communicate it?
35. How do you **verify identity** before revealing information to a client?
36. What is your **success rate**, and how do you define "success"?
37. Do you have a **case management system**? Is client data encrypted?
38. Who has **access to client files**? Are collaborators bound by confidentiality agreements?
39. How long do you **retain** case files? How can a client request deletion?
40. How do you handle **payments** (methods, currency, deposits, installments, invoices)?

---

## D. Pricing

41. Do you publish **prices**? If not, why not?
42. What is the **range** for a family search, an infidelity case, a child-support location, a vehicle recovery, a corporate investigation?
43. Is the **first consultation free**? Is there a diagnostic fee?
44. Do you charge **only on success** for any service? Which?
45. Are there **additional costs** (travel, DNA lab, legal fees, court fees)?
46. Do you offer **payment plans**?
47. How do you handle **refunds** if the case cannot proceed?
48. Do you have a **written contract / engagement letter** the site can reference?

---

## E. Proof and content

49. Which **testimonials are real**, and do you have written consent to publish them?
50. Can you provide **10–20 case stories** with: year, relationship, country, outcome, quote, consent status?
51. Do you have **photos/videos of real reunions** we can use? With consent?
52. Can we **export the Facebook reviews** (13 on the main page, 15 on the legacy page)?
53. Do you have **press mentions, TV interviews, awards, or institutional recognitions**?
54. Which **numbers** are approved for the site: years, cases, families, collaborators, countries?
55. Can we **remove "100% efectividad"** and replace it with a defensible claim?
56. What is the **story behind the gold globe logo**? Can we redraw it as SVG?
57. Do you have a **higher-resolution version** of the directors' photo? A team photo?
58. Can we photograph the **office, the team at work, documents/maps** (without client data)?
59. Who wrote the old case stories? Can we reuse them with edits?
60. Are there **client stories we must never publish**?

---

## F. Brand and language

61. What is the **one name** you want to be known by? ("BuscandoPersonas.org", "BuscandoPersonas Sin Fronteras", "Buscándote"?)
62. Do you want to keep **"Sin Fronteras"** in the name?
63. Is the brand **religious**? Should the site use faith language, or keep it only in testimonials?
64. Do you actually serve clients in **English and Italian**? Who speaks them?
65. Is there demand from **Italy, Spain, the US, Chile, Argentina, Ecuador**? Which market matters most?
66. Do you want **tú** or **usted** on the site?
67. Are there words you **never** want used ("detective", "espía", "rastreo", "cazar")?
68. Do you want to keep the **"Designed by Enmanuel"** credit?

---

## G. Privacy, ethics and risk

69. Do you have a **privacy policy** today? Can we draft one from your actual practices?
70. What **legal basis** do you rely on to process sensitive personal data?
71. How do you obtain **consent from the person being sought** before contact?
72. What is your policy on **contacting someone against their will**?
73. How do you protect **minors and vulnerable adults**?
74. What is your policy on **illegal methods** (hacking, phone tapping, unauthorised database access)?
75. How do you handle a client who asks for something **unethical or illegal**?
76. Have you had **complaints, disputes, or legal claims**? How were they resolved?
77. How do you prevent **fraud against your own clients** (fake investigators, impersonation of your brand)?
78. Do you have an **ethics code** or a complaints channel?
79. Are you comfortable publishing the **office address** and **full names**?
80. Do you want to publish **guarantees**? What can you legally promise?

---

## H. Website and operations

81. Who **owns the domain** and the Firebase project?
82. Who **deploys** the site today? Is there a staging environment?
83. Do you want a **CMS** (WordPress, Sanity, etc.) or a static site?
84. Who will **update case stories and blog posts** after launch?
85. Do you have a **Google Business Profile**? Who manages it?
86. Do you have **Google Analytics / Search Console**? Any historical data?
87. What **CRM** or inbox receives the form submissions?
88. Do you want **live chat**, or is WhatsApp enough?
89. Do you need **online payments** or **appointment booking**?
90. What is the **budget and timeline** for the redesign?

---

## I. The five questions that matter most

If time is short, ask these first:

1. **What is the legal entity and the approved founding year?**
2. **Which numbers can we publish, and who approves them?**
3. **Which testimonials and case stories are real and consented?**
4. **What is the actual process, timeline and price range after someone contacts you?**
5. **What is the one name and one phone number you want the world to use?**

---

## How to record the answers

Create `context/answers/` and one file per interview or topic. Update the relevant context file (not the master brief only) so the whole pack stays consistent. Mark each answer with the date and the person who gave it.
